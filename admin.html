Map Manager

Drop in your maps, say which room and frame each one belongs to, then download one file and upload it to GitHub. No code needed. Nothing is uploaded from this page; everything stays on your computer until you upload it yourself.

2. Say where each belongsPick a room (and a frame group in City) and press Place. You can reorder or move maps later.
3. Download and uploadPress “Download update”, unzip it, then drag the files into your GitHub repository.
